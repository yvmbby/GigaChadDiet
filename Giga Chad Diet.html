How the recommendation works

The planner builds two main meals per day using your Excel prices and selects a weekly protein rotation for each strategy. It rounds purchases up to full packages and includes potatoes, produce, dairy and eggs. Steak and fish switches modify the rotation.

The calorie estimate uses the Mifflin–St Jeor equation for males, an activity multiplier and a selected surplus. Height and weight affect calorie needs; they do not automatically make an ingredient cheaper.

Meal ideas and shopping costs are planning estimates, not a verified calorie-complete or protein-complete diet. Adjust portions, fruit, potatoes and dairy to meet targets; verify with nutrition labels.
